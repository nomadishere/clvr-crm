Questions

Straight answers.

How accurate are the measurements?

Roofly uses Google's roof data and high-resolution aerial imagery, then AI traces the edges. You can drag any edge to fix it, and every change re-saves to the job. For insurance claims where an adjuster asks for a specific report brand, you can still order one. Most retail jobs won't need it.

Can I bring my data from JobNimbus, AccuLynx or a spreadsheet?

Yes. Send us your export or spreadsheet and we'll load your contacts, jobs and open leads during setup.

Does it work on my phone?

It was designed on a phone first. Open it in Safari or Chrome and add it to your home screen, and it runs like an app, in day or night mode.

Who owns my data?

You do. Your company's data lives in its own secure database with row-level security, and you can export it whenever you like.

What does it cost?

We're onboarding a small group of founding roofing companies first, with founding-crew pricing and no per-report measurement fees. Book a demo and we'll walk you through it.

I'm already on the team. Where do I log in?

Tap Log in at the top of this page, or go to clvrcrm.com/login. Save it to your home screen and it opens straight into the CRM.
